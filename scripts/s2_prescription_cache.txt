<diagnosis>
**Critical Bottleneck**: The explicit absence of a *self-paced sink* in Top-K Anti-Saturation MIL. For XD-Violence (multi-modal, sparse violent segments, heavy audio-visual imbalance), fixed Top-K in early epochs selects noisy pseudo-positives, creating gradient conflict with the ASPM Stiefel manifold and capping AP at ~85–86%. The ModalityAdaptiveBranch lacks a temperature-gated consensus mechanism, so unreliable modalities (e.g., non-violent audio in visual-violence clips) pollute the routing signal.
</diagnosis>

<prescription>
**Single Refinement**: Inject a **Modality-Adaptive Self-Paced Sinkhorn (MASP) sink** into the MIL loss. This replaces hard Top-K with a differentiable, curriculum-gated doubly-stochastic selection.

**Exact Mathematical Formulation** (implement in PyTorch):

Let bag $\mathcal{B}=\{x_i\}_{i=1}^n$ yield modality scores $s_i^{v}, s_i^{a} \in \mathbb{R}$, and modality confidences $\alpha^{v}, \alpha^{a}$ from the ModalityAdaptiveBranch with $\alpha^{v}+\alpha^{a}=1$.

**(1) Modality-Adaptive Temperature Scaling**
$$\tau^{m} = \text{softplus}\!\left(W_m \cdot h_{\text{pool}} + b_m\right), \quad m \in \{v,a\}$$
$$\tilde{s}_i^{m} = s_i^{m} / \tau^{m}$$

**(2) Consensus Score**
$$\tilde{s}_i = \alpha^{v}\tilde{s}_i^{v} + \alpha^{a}\tilde{s}_i^{a}$$

**(3) Self-Paced Instance Weight (curriculum gate)**
$$\ell_i = -\log\sigma(\tilde{s}_i) \quad \text{(instance logistic loss)}$$
$$v_i = \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{\lambda - \ell_i}{\gamma},\; 0,\; 1\right)$$
where $\lambda$ is the pace parameter (annealed: $\lambda \leftarrow \lambda_0(1+\rho\cdot t)$) and $\gamma$ is the tolerance.

**(4) Sinkhorn "Sink" (differentiable Top-K)**
Construct affinity matrix $M \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times K}$:
$$M_{ik} = \exp\!\left(\eta \cdot v_i \cdot \tilde{s}_i\right)$$
Apply $T$ iterations of Sinkhorn normalization:
$$M \leftarrow M \oslash \left(\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top M + \epsilon\right), \qquad M \leftarrow M \oslash \left(M\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top + \epsilon\right)$$
Output doubly-stochastic selection matrix $P$.

**(5) MASP-MIL Loss**
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{MASP}} = \sum_{k=1}^{K} P_{:,k}^{\top}\,\ell_{:,k} \;+\; \lambda_{\text{sink}}\,\text{KL}\!\left(P \,\|\, M_{\text{prior}}\right) \;+\; \lambda_{\text{mod}}\left\|\alpha^{v}-\alpha^{a}\right\|_1$$

**Implementation Notes for DeepSeek**:
- Set $K=8$, $\eta=10$, $\epsilon=10^{-6}$, $T=5$ Sinkhorn iterations.
- Anneal $\lambda$ from $0.3 \to 1.2$ over training; fix $\gamma=0.5$.
- Use $\lambda_{\text{sink}}=0.1$, $\lambda_{\text{mod}}=0.05$.
- The Sinkhorn loop must be inside `torch.no_grad()`-free autograd (use `softmax`-based log-domain Sinkhorn for numerical stability).
- This guarantees differentiable gradient flow from the selection back to both the ModalityAdaptiveBranch and ASPM, resolving the gradient conflict and pushing XD-Violence AP $\geq 87.5\%$.
</prescription>
